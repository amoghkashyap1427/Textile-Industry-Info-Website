About Us

Textile Mastery in the Heart of Surat

Uday Creation is a Surat-based cloth job-work specialist trusted by manufacturers, designers, and traders across the textile industry. We bring precision and craftsmanship to every order — from intricate embossing to flowing long crush finishes.

Founded on the principles of quality and reliability, we have grown to become one of Surat's most dependable job-work partners. Our facility is equipped with modern machinery and operated by skilled craftspeople who understand the nuances of every fabric type.

Whether you need a small batch or large-scale production, we deliver consistency, precision, and on-time fulfilment — every single time.

What We Do

Our Services

Six specialised textile treatments — precision-executed at scale, delivered on time.

Crushing

Fabric crushing for textured, crinkled effects that add dimension and character to any cloth.

Embossing

Raised pattern imprinting on fabric surfaces — precise, defined, and visually striking.

Pleating

Precision pleating in various styles and widths — knife, box, accordion — crafted with consistency.

Sparkle

Glitter and shimmer finishing on fabric — adding brilliance and visual flair to garments.

Fusing

Bonding of interfacing layers with precision — adding structure and body to fabric for superior garment construction.

Long Crush

Extended crush for flowing, crumpled aesthetics — ideal for sarees, dupattas, and designer fabrics.
